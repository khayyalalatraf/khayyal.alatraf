Text (quoted from PDF):
> The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of the Real Estate General Authority (REGA),
> ... between Lessor and Lessee, issued under the Royal Decree No. (M/73), dated 02/04/1447 AH.,
> First: Notwithstanding the period stated in paragraph (1) of Article (VIII) of the Legal Provisions
> Regulating Relationship between Lessor and Lessee, the notice period, given by a lessor to a
> lessee for unwillingness to renew a lease agreement in the standard agreement for residential real estates,
> shall be increased to not less than (365) days before the expiry date of a lease agreement,
> if a lessee desires not to renew the lease agreement in order to vacate the real estate for their personal use,
> or for use by one of their fist-degree relative.
> Second: This Resolution shall be published in official gazette and shall be applicable as of its [publication date]
Signed: CEO of REGA, Abdullah bin Soud Alhammad.
The PDF states NO number and NO date for this resolution itself.

## 2) Follow-up REGA decision that states the date of (1)

- Page: https://rega.gov.sa/en/laws-and-decisions/regulations-and-by-laws/controls/decision-announcing-the-extension-of-the-notice-period-for-residential-properties-to-365-days-in-cases-of-non-renewal-for-personal-use/
- PDF: https://rega.gov.sa/media/fjtbphnq/decision-announcing-the-extension-of-the-notice-period-for-residential-properties-to-365-days-in-cases-of-non-renewal-for-personal-use.pdf
- Listing date 1447/09/13.

Exact quote from PDF:
> based on the Resolution No. (470000480), dated 02/07/1447 AH,

Content (summary): the 365-day notice period in "the aforesaid Resolution" applies to all residential leases
in force when the Legal Provisions took effect; if the 365-day notice is not met, the lease is renewed for the
period needed to complete it, counted from service of the non-renewal notice; published in the gazette, effective on its date.
